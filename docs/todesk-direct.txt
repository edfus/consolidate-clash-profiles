rules/ToDesk.yml is embedded first in meta/mobile/tun templates and in consolidate(), before Anthropic, profile/injection rules and generic providers. Domains use exact suffix matching. The process list covers native ToDesk and common service/session/lite/setup/remote-assistance executables, including Unix names without .exe. No broad keyword, shared CDN domain or guessed IP range is included. Generic processes retain existing routing.

find-process-mode: strict is already enabled in all three templates. Router-forwarded traffic cannot reliably identify an originating application on another device; domain matching still works, while arbitrary peer IPs require local process identification. This change does not guarantee that every remote session becomes peer-to-peer; DIRECT means Mihomo does not send the matched traffic through a proxy.
